Our Programs

Odong League

Indonesia's Premier Youth Football League

Odong League is our flagship football competition that brings together talented young athletes from across Indonesia. The league provides a platform for players to showcase their skills, learn from experienced coaches, and compete at a high level.

With multiple participating clubs and teams, Odong League creates an environment where young athletes can develop not only their football abilities but also important life skills such as teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship.

  • Professional league structure
  • Experienced coaches and referees
  • Regular season and playoffs
  • Talent scouting opportunities
  • Live streaming of matches

MyCampus League

University Sports Competition

MyCampus League is designed for university students who want to continue their athletic journey while pursuing higher education. This program recognizes that academic excellence and athletic achievement go hand in hand.

The league fosters inter-university competition, building camaraderie among students from different institutions while promoting a healthy balance between studies and sports.

  • Multi-sport competition
  • University partnerships
  • Academic support programs
  • Networking opportunities
  • Scholarship opportunities

Sport Leadership Academy

Developing Tomorrow's Leaders

The Sport Leadership Academy is our comprehensive leadership development program that uses sports as a vehicle to teach essential leadership skills. Participants learn about communication, decision-making, team management, and ethical leadership.

Through workshops, mentorship, and practical experience, academy graduates are prepared to become leaders in their communities, whether in sports, business, or social initiatives.

  • Leadership workshops
  • One-on-one mentorship
  • Community project management
  • Public speaking training
  • Certification upon completion
