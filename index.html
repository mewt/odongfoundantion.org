About Our Foundation

Empowering Youth Through Sports

ODONG Foundation is dedicated to nurturing the potential of Indonesian youth through innovative sports and educational programs that build character, leadership, and community.

Our Mission

To empower Indonesian youth through sports-based programs that develop physical, mental, and leadership skills for lifelong success.

Our Vision

A future where every young Indonesian has access to quality sports education and opportunities to reach their full potential.

Our Values

Integrity, teamwork, excellence, and community development guide everything we do at ODONG Foundation.
