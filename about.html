About Us

Learn about our mission, vision, and the people behind ODONG Foundation

Our Mission

To empower Indonesian youth through sports programs that build character, develop leadership skills, and create opportunities for personal and community growth.

Our Vision

To be a leading force in youth development across Indonesia, creating a generation of confident, skilled, and compassionate leaders who positively impact their communities.

Our Story

ODONG Foundation was founded with a simple yet powerful belief: that every young person in Indonesia deserves the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Through our various programs including Odong League, MyCampus League, and the Sport Leadership Academy, we have been working tirelessly to create pathways for youth development through sports.

Our foundation is registered and legally recognized, operating with transparency and accountability in all our endeavors. We partner with schools, universities, communities, and corporations to maximize our impact.

Our Values
